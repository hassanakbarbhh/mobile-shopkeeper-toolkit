Privacy Policy — Mobile Shopkeeper Toolkit
1. We collect only: account identifiers (email or phone via Firebase Authentication)
   and the business records you enter (products, sales, customers, repairs, expenses).
2. Business records are stored on your device by default. If you enable cloud sync,
   records are stored in the developer's Firebase project, encrypted in transit.
3. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for advertising.
   No ad SDKs and no trackers beyond Google Firebase infrastructure.
4. You can delete your account and cloud data at any time from
   Settings → Delete my account, or by emailing hassanakbarbhh@gmail.com.
   Deletion completes within 30 days.
5. This app is not directed to children under 13.
6. Policy changes will be posted in the app's GitHub repository.
